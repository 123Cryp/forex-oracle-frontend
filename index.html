A minimal dApp for ForexCrossRateOracle — the two-party, address-bound, deadline-gated, multi-source forex settlement contract on GenLayer. Create an agreement, have the counterparty accept it, submit sources for resolution once the deadline arrives, and read back the deterministic verdict straight from validator consensus.

01 Create Agreement

You (the connected wallet) automatically become party_a. Enter the counterparty's address as party_b — they must separately accept before this binds them.

Converted to UTC automatically. Must be at least 5 minutes from now.

Allowed: xe.com, oanda.com, investing.com, tradingeconomics.com, x-rates.com, fxstreet.com, bloomberg.com, reuters.com, wsj.com, marketwatch.com, forex.com, wise.com, dailyfx.com, exchangerates.org.uk, federalreserve.gov, bankofengland.co.uk

02 Accept / Cancel Agreement

accept_agreement must be called by the wallet set as party_b. cancel_agreement must be called by party_a, and only while still awaiting acceptance.

03 Resolve Agreement

Only callable at or after the resolution deadline, and before the resolution window closes. Submit 2–6 candidate source URLs; every domain committed at creation time must be represented.

04 Expire Agreement

Permissionless cleanup for lapsed agreements: never accepted before the deadline, or never resolved before the resolution window closed.

05 Read Agreement

Pull the full auditable record for any agreement ID, straight from contract storage. No wallet needed.

06 Check Role

Look up whether an address is party_a, party_b, or unrelated to a given agreement.

07 Total Agreements

Total number of agreements ever created on this contract instance.
